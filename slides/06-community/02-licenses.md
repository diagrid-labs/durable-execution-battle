# Open source licenses

| License | What it is | Advantages | Risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Apache 2.0** | Permissive license with an explicit patent grant | Use, modify & redistribute freely (incl. closed-source); built-in patent protection; enterprise-friendly | Almost none — no copyleft, no commercial restrictions |
| **MIT** | Minimal permissive license | Maximal freedom; tiny, easy to understand; broad compatibility | No explicit patent grant; no protection if a competitor forks it as a SaaS |
| **BSL 1.1** | "Source-available" — converts to an open license after a set period | Source is visible & usable; protects vendor from cloud providers reselling it; **reverts to Apache 2.0 after 4 years** | **Not OSI-approved**; production/competing-use restrictions until conversion; needs legal review |
